# Research Plan: Comprehensive SaaS Strategic Analysis for AI Salon Booking Platform

## Objectives
- To provide a deep strategic analysis covering SuperSaaS reseller model, multi-tenant SaaS architecture, and emergency MVP strategies for salon booking systems.
- To deliver actionable insights for the immediate onboarding of "InStyle Hair Boutique" and a long-term platform strategy.

## Research Breakdown
- **SuperSaaS Analysis**:
  - Analyze SuperSaaS API documentation and reseller capabilities.
  - Evaluate the pros and cons of the SuperSaaS reseller model.
- **SaaS Architecture**:
  - Research multi-tenant SaaS architecture patterns for booking systems.
  - Investigate hybrid wrapper architecture patterns for gradual platform migration.
- **Emergency MVP Strategy**:
  - Identify MVP strategies that demonstrate immediate value to salon clients.
  - Analyze WhatsApp Business API integration patterns for appointment booking.
- **Market & Competitive Analysis**:
  - Research salon industry-specific pain points and high-value features.
  - Conduct a competitive analysis of existing salon booking SaaS solutions.

## Key Questions
1. What are the specific capabilities and limitations of the SuperSaaS API for a salon booking system?
2. What are the most effective multi-tenant architecture patterns for a scalable and secure salon booking platform?
3. What is the fastest way to deliver a functional MVP to "InStyle Hair Boutique" while building a custom solution?
4. What are the technical requirements and best practices for integrating WhatsApp Business API for appointment booking?
5. What are the most pressing needs of salon owners that can be addressed with high-value features?
6. Who are the leading competitors in the salon booking SaaS market, and what are their key differentiators?
7. How can a hybrid architecture be leveraged to migrate from a reseller model to a fully custom platform with minimal disruption?

## Resource Strategy
- **Primary data sources**: Web search, developer documentation (SuperSaaS, WhatsApp), and industry reports.
- **Search strategies**: Utilize targeted keywords such as "SuperSaaS API documentation", "multi-tenant SaaS architecture booking system", "salon management software market analysis", and "WhatsApp Business API for booking".

## Verification Plan
- **Source requirements**: Triangulate information from at least 3-5 independent and credible sources for each major research area.
- **Cross-validation**: Compare and contrast findings from different sources to ensure accuracy and identify any conflicting information.

## Expected Deliverables
- A comprehensive strategic analysis report in Markdown format, addressing all research areas.
- Actionable recommendations for both short-term (emergency MVP) and long-term (platform development) strategies.
- The final report will be located at `docs/strategic_analysis.md`.

## Workflow Selection
- **Primary focus**: Search-focused
- **Justification**: The task requires extensive information gathering and synthesis from a wide range of external sources to provide a comprehensive analysis.
